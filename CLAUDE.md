# Project: 荒原嘉年华 · 四驱越野 (Wildland Festival ATV)

Browser 3D open-world ATV racing game. three.js r159 UMD build loaded by classic scripts, no build step:
double-clicking `index.html` runs it. All art and sound are procedural; ground and foliage textures are
generated by `tools/textures.py` and embedded as data URIs in `js/textures.js` (Chrome refuses local image
files as WebGL textures under `file://`).

## TL;DR
2 km × 2 km heightfield (rolling hills, border mountains, a lake, festival plateau) with a 3.1 km dirt trail
loop, 7 kicker ramps, a speed trap and a jump-distance sign. Custom rigid-body ATV physics (four raycast
suspensions, slip-limited tyres, attitude hold in the air). Free roam, a 12-checkpoint race started from the
pink marker at the festival, skill chains (air, drift, near miss, speed, off-road). Rendering goes through an
HDR pipeline in `js/post.js` (bloom, ACES, grading, FXAA, dynamic resolution).

## Testing with Claude in Chrome
- Serve over http (the extension cannot open `file://`): `node tools/serve.mjs 8840 <shotsDir>`, open `http://localhost:8840/`.
- Extension tabs are hidden, so rAF never fires. `DBG.take()` stops the loop; `DBG.run(sec, input, {fast})`,
  `DBG.until(cond)`, `DBG.at(s, driveSeconds)`, `DBG.lap()` (bot race), `DBG.refresh()` then a screenshot.
  `DBG.hook()` + `DBG.report()` log HUD messages, flips and NaN. `await DBG.shot(name)` saves a full-res JPEG.
- GPU cost: render N frames and finish with `gl.readPixels` before stopping the clock; bisect by toggling features.
  This machine renders through ANGLE/D3D11 on an Intel UHD (0x9A60) iGPU.

## Traps (verified)
- three r159 applies fog *after* tone mapping and colour-space conversion. Here everything renders into a linear
  HDR target, the fog chunks are replaced (`world.js`) and share `hazeColor()` with the sky, so distant land
  meets the horizon exactly.
- three r159 `WebGLShadowMap` tests `object.layers` against the **view** camera, not the shadow camera.
  Shadow-only casters (layer 1) are drawn by `World.renderShadows()`: a render with an empty-frustum camera that
  enables layer 1, then the main pass reuses the map (`shadowMap.autoUpdate = false`).
- `HTMLImageElement.decode()` never resolves in a hidden tab; texture loading waits on `onload`.
- Half-float targets overflow to Inf on sharp clear-coat glints; the bloom prefilter turns Inf into NaN and the
  blur spreads one NaN pixel over the whole frame (black screen). Every bloom tap and the composite clamp and
  drop NaN.
- Clamping the trail height to the lake level everywhere raised it 13 m over low valleys with 50° banks that
  trap the ATV; the clamp is local to the lake and shoulders widen with the height difference.
- Splat shader skips zero-weight layers and distant normal maps; derivatives are taken before the branches and
  sampled with `textureGrad`, otherwise mip selection breaks at branch boundaries.
- Foliage cards are double-sided with outward (spherised) normals; the default back-face normal flip is removed
  so both sides light the same. Impostor targets clear to foliage green with zero alpha to avoid dark mip halos.
- The extension's `zoom` screenshot returns black for the WebGL canvas; use a full screenshot or `DBG.shot`.
